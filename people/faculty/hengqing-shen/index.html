Biography

Hengqing Shen earned his Ph.D. in environmental science at Peking University. He studies how emissions from the ocean and subsequent reactions in air and particles affect oxidation and secondary pollution in coastal regions.

By combining field measurements, laboratory experiments, and modeling, he investigates the marine sources and transformations of nitrous acid (HONO), reactive halogens, and carbonyl compounds. His work connects ocean–atmosphere exchange with the cycling of reactive carbon, nitrogen, and halogens.

Research Interests

  • Emission of reactive gases from the ocean and their effects on atmospheric chemistry
  • Multiphase reactions of reactive nitrogen, halogens, and carbonyl compounds
  • Atmospheric oxidation and secondary pollutant formation in coastal environments
