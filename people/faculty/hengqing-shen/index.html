Hengqing Shen, Ph.D.

Research Professor

Environment Research Institute, Shandong University

Email
hqshen@sdu.edu.cn

Biography

Hengqing Shen earned his Ph.D. at Peking University. He combines field observations, laboratory experiments, and modeling to study marine emissions and multiphase reactions involving reactive nitrogen, halogens, and carbonyl compounds. His work examines how this chemistry affects atmospheric oxidation and secondary pollution in coastal regions.
