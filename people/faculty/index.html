Faculty

Yuqiang Zhang, Ph.D.

Professor

Email yuqiang.zhang@sdu.edu.cn

Yuqiang Zhang earned his Ph.D. at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. He combines atmospheric modeling and satellite data to study how climate policy, pollutant transport, and urbanization affect air quality and human health.

Qinyi Li, Ph.D.

Professor

Email qinyi.li@sdu.edu.cn

Qinyi Li earned his Ph.D. at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University. He develops regional and global atmospheric models to study how reactive halogens and nitrogen affect air quality and climate.

Liubin Huang, Ph.D.

Research Professor

Email hliubin@sdu.edu.cn

Liubin Huang earned his Ph.D. at Peking University. He studies multiphase aerosol chemistry, the formation and transformation of secondary aerosols, and the environmental and climatic effects of marine aerosols.

Zheng Zong, Ph.D.

Research Professor

Email zzong@sdu.edu.cn

Zheng Zong earned his Ph.D. at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. He uses stable isotopes to trace the sources and formation pathways of atmospheric nitrate, ammonium, and nitrous acid.

Hengqing Shen, Ph.D.

Research Professor

Email hqshen@sdu.edu.cn

Hengqing Shen earned his Ph.D. at Peking University. He combines field observations, laboratory experiments, and modeling to study how marine reactive gases affect coastal atmospheric oxidation and secondary pollution.

Yujiao Zhu, Ph.D.

Associate Research Professor

Email zhuyujiao@sdu.edu.cn

Yujiao Zhu earned her Ph.D. at Ocean University of China. She studies atmospheric particle formation and growth, including how marine aerosols and newly formed particles influence clouds and climate.
