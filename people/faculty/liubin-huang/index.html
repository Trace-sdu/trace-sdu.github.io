Liubin Huang, Ph.D.

Research Professor

Environment Research Institute, Shandong University

Email
hliubin@sdu.edu.cn

Biography

Liubin Huang earned his Ph.D. at Peking University. He studies multiphase aerosol chemistry, how secondary aerosols form and change, and the effects of marine aerosols on the atmosphere and climate.

Education and Appointments
