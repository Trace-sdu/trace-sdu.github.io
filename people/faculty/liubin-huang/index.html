Biography

Liubin Huang earned his Ph.D. in environmental science at Peking University. He studies reactions at the interface between gases and particles and how those reactions affect the composition and evolution of atmospheric aerosols.

His work combines laboratory studies and atmospheric observations to identify how organic peroxides contribute to secondary particle formation and how biogenic and human sources contribute to atmospheric organosulfates. He also investigates the chemistry of marine aerosols and its environmental and climatic effects.

Research Interests

  • Multiphase aerosol chemistry and heterogeneous reaction mechanisms
  • Formation and transformation of secondary aerosols
  • Environmental and climatic effects of marine aerosols
