Biography

Yuqiang Zhang earned his Ph.D. in environmental science and engineering at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. At Shandong University, he uses global and regional climate and chemical transport models, satellite retrievals, and machine learning to assess how emissions policies affect air quality and health. His work examines how China can meet its carbon peaking and carbon neutrality goals while improving air quality.

He serves on the standing committees for ozone pollution and artificial intelligence of the Chinese Society for Environmental Sciences. He is also an editorial board member of Earth System Science Data.

Research Interests

  • Air quality and health benefits of greenhouse gas mitigation
  • Long-range and transboundary transport of air pollutants
  • Wet and dry atmospheric deposition
  • Urbanization, urban heat islands, and air quality
  • Satellite retrievals and machine learning for atmospheric analysis
