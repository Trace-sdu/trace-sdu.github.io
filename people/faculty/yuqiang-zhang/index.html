Yuqiang Zhang, Ph.D.

Professor

Environment Research Institute, Shandong University

Email
yuqiang.zhang@sdu.edu.cn

Biography

Yuqiang Zhang earned his Ph.D. at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. He uses atmospheric modeling and data science to study how climate change and air pollution affect human health, with particular interests in pollutant transport, atmospheric deposition, and urban air quality.
