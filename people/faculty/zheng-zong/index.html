Biography

Zheng Zong earned his Ph.D. in environmental science at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences. He uses stable isotope measurements and source apportionment models to determine where air pollutants come from and how they form. His research focuses on the atmospheric reactive nitrogen cycle, including particulate nitrate, ammonium, and nitrous acid (HONO).

He has led projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the China Postdoctoral Science Foundation. He serves on the early-career editorial board of Earth and Environment and has been a guest editor for an Atmosphere special issue on isotope-based source tracing.

Research Interests

  • Stable isotope tracing of atmospheric reactive nitrogen
  • Sources and formation mechanisms of particulate nitrate, ammonium, and HONO
  • Isotope fractionation during nitrogen transformations and source apportionment in coastal and urban air
